Wellington

A curated guide to the culinary capital. From windswept coasts to hidden laneways, discover the places we love most in the city we call home.

flight

Arriving

Wellington Airport (WLG) is conveniently located just 15 minutes from the city center. Taxis and ride-shares are readily available outside the terminal.

directions_car

Transport

The Airport Express Bus ($9) departs every 20 minutes.
